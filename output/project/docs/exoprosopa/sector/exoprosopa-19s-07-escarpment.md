# <img src="/exoprosopa/images/sector-exoprosopa-19s-07-escarpment.png" width="48" height="48"></img> Escarpment
An important transportation route between outposts used by Rhodes.
To make up for the lack of tantrium in the area, research the [accent]Tantraformer to create tantrium as the replacement.

Rhodes does not take kindly to you being here and will do their utmost to stop you.
