    Azimuth

    Property Value
    Author DeerCarbonate
    Repository https://github.com/deercarbonate/azimuth
    Stars 47
    Last updated 2026-07-13T06:37:39Z

    Hybrid mod primarily built around a gradually branching campaign that expands in multiple directions.

    The mod adds a new planet, Gelion, featuring its own campaign, star system, research tree, and gameplay mechanics.
    The planet offers over 90 blocks to research, 12 units each with their own unique abilities, 12 campaign sectors, more than 6 new mechanics, and plenty of room for map creation.

    On top of that, the campaign also stands out with absolutely BRUTAL visual effects. Every texture was made with care and dedication.

    THIS MOD IS STILL IN DEVELOPMENT!
    The current completion percentage is approximately 57%, with plenty of room to grow!

    Help me develop the mod! Join the Azimuth Discord server using the link in the README file on the mod's official GitHub page.

    ///////////////////////////////

    Land on a sub-cryonic terra slowly heating up beneath its unstable star. The celestial body is suffering from global warming — glaciers are melting, atmospheric pressure is rising, and temperatures continue to climb.
    Exterminate the enemy faction. Turn the planet into a temporary source of raw materials. Witness a world that will soon become ash.

    If you enjoyed the mod, please consider giving it a star!
    Thank you for playing.
