    Serpulo+

    Property Value
    Author ThePullerCell
    Repository https://github.com/traincrisis/serpuloplus
    Stars 17
    Last updated 2026-05-28T16:31:25Z

    A massive personalized content expansion for Serpulo.

    Most of the content in this mod is my vision for what I wanted to be in the game, yet isn't.

    Content included:

    • 2 more tiers for every vanilla crafter

    • The Alloy Trio, composed of Neoplastic, Cryogenic and Ultimatter alloys, each having its own large set of blocks made out of them

    • Four new fluids: Lubricant, Steam, Lava, and Quantofluid, plus a new ingredient called Magmatite

    • Liquid capsules, which allow for the transportation of liquids in the form of items, for things like cross-sector transportation via export, or long range transportation over terrain via mass driver.

    Credits:

    • Myself obviously, for implementing all the content in the mod.

    • red.id9990 for spriting the vanilla crafter upgrades

    • tinnphat_ for spriting almost every remaining block

    • giga_chad96 for the sprite of Voltage, and Tesla
