    ⚠ PERFORMANCE WARNING: This mod may crash or misbehave on weaker PCs and phones.
    Best played with a completely fresh campaign save.

    Currently in development. Description will try to increase player engagement later.
    Allure is a large RTS mod that attempts to bring a true start-to-finish storyline to the table. And also hardcore gameplay. Enjoy being swarmed by the bee legion.

    This mod is planned to introduce an overhauled vanilla experience, with sweeping changes across every aspect of Serpulo. Maps are redone, tutorials added.
    What comes after? Well, I guess we'll have to find out. I'm not giving you any spoilers. You'll find out if you're curious.

    Enabling this mod will break every Serpulo schematic until disabled. Be careful, well, if you like yours.

    Works on beta build 157. Edit the mod.hjson's minGameVersion to use.
